Research red flags and what to check

Use these cues to decide what evidence to collect. Several cues extend beyond the recorded teaching set. Each needs its own passage, target, and context.

Inherited collective blame

A sweeping accusation moves from an alleged act to Jews as a group.

In Plzeň, inspect the group label and the collective curse. Record the reported speaker before coding.

Jerusalem Declaration on Antisemitism, especially guidelines 1–5 and 6–15

Collective responsibility for Israel

A statement transfers the acts of a state or individual to Jewish people.

Identify who did the alleged act and who is blamed. Look for an explicit transfer of responsibility.

The Nexus Document on antisemitism, Israel, and Zionism

Conspiracy and coded proxies

A claim describes a hidden Jewish network as controlling public affairs.

Ask how the wording makes the Jewish target and claim of coordinated control identifiable. Test plausible alternative readings of a code or name.

Zannettou and colleagues, A Quantitative Approach to Understanding Online Antisemitism

Dual loyalty or presumed agency

A person is presumed to work for a foreign government because they are Jewish.

Separate evidence about this individual’s actions from an assumption attached to identity.

The Nexus Document on antisemitism, Israel, and Zionism

Dehumanization, exclusion, or threats

A text denies equal standing, depicts Jews as subhuman, or targets people or institutions as Jewish.

Locate the target, the proposed treatment, and whether the speaker endorses, describes, or condemns it.

IHRA working definition and contextual examples

Blood libel

A specific false accusation alleges that Jews murder non-Jews for ritual purposes.

Check whether the ritual allegation is present. The reported Plzeň wording makes a different religious accusation.

US Holocaust Memorial Museum, Blood Libel

Holocaust denial or distortion

A text denies established events, radically minimizes them, or uses misleading historical claims.

Preserve the asserted historical proposition and source. A reference to the Holocaust can also report an allegation or commemorate a survivor.

US Holocaust Memorial Museum, Holocaust Denial key dates

Political target and surrounding context

Language about Israel or Zionism can cross into anti-Jewish hostility through its target or tropes.

Identify the policy, institution, or people being targeted. Apply the selected framework’s context rules and keep its boundary result separate.

Jerusalem Declaration on Antisemitism, especially guidelines 1–5 and 6–15

Misassigned voice

A quote, denunciation, translation, or reported claim is treated as the reporter’s view.

Track the attribution verb, quote boundary, surrounding paragraph, and stance toward the exact proposition.

Röttger and colleagues, HateCheck functional tests
