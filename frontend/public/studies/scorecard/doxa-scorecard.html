  • Documented safety risk · The source explicitly cites a record or measurement of concrete emergency-access harm, such as a documented ambulance delay. A proposed risk, a reference to an available traffic plan, or a call for future measurements by itself does not meet this criterion.
  • Favorable appraisal of restrictions · The source favorably characterizes the proposed two-block exclusion zone as a safety or public-order measure. A negative appraisal, neutral mention, or quoting an opponent's language without endorsement does not meet this criterion.
  • Restriction as remedy · The source recommends adopting the proposed two-block exclusion zone to address a safety or public-order problem. Recommendations limited to emergency lanes, stewards, or data collection do not meet this criterion.
  • Prior controls presumed ineffective · The text presents failure of existing controls as a taken-for-granted premise of the proposed measure. If wording such as restore order lacks a clear connection to a specific prior control or is explicitly questioned, preserve uncertainty.
