Build an inquiry another reader can inspect. This map follows the actual DOXA workflow, from a document library to a downloadable research record.

1Import documents

Load the public research collection, or add your own PDF, DOCX, text, Markdown and saved HTML. Inspect extraction quality and correct author, institution, date and type. Metadata CSV helps with larger collections.

Large uploads resume in bounded parts. Select the same file after an interruption.

Open the library →

2Choose your lens

State a research question and select explicit frameworks. Compare IHRA, Jerusalem and Nexus separately, explore anti-Muslim prejudice, or write your own criteria for language norms and other topics.

Reviewer priorities and research, briefing or teaching profiles are declared perspectives.

Define an inquiry →

3Run the engine

Choose private local Ollama, configured cloud inference, or the no-model lexical baseline. Check readiness, set a budget and follow the run monitor. You can inspect the saved showcase without making any new inference calls.

Word matches do not establish prejudice. Remote inference sends selected source text to that provider.

Choose an engine →

4Check the evidence

Open an observation to see its exact highlighted passage, speaker attribution, criterion, explanation and alternative reading. Read the context; then confirm, reject or annotate it.

Reporting, quotation, rebuttal and hypothetical examples require different interpretations.

Inspect the sources →

5Compare findings

Explore author and institutional patterns, framework differences and imported-batch changes. Keep an eye on coverage: missing analysis cannot establish an absence of observations.

A source missing from an incremental import is not automatically a deleted source.

Compare perspectives →

6Export the research

Choose research, briefing or teaching emphasis, then download PDF, HTML, XLSX, CSV or JSONL. Reports preserve the selected run, filters, source references and methodology.

PDF/HTML identify their evidence-appendix limits. Data exports retain the complete selection.

Make a report →
