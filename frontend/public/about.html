What is doxa?

Doxa refers to opinion, common belief or what is taken for granted. DOXA helps investigate the language through which documents assert, assume, normalize and contest ideas. It connects an interpretation to a specific passage, an explicit analytical lens and a review record.

“Bias Detection as a Service” describes an open-source toolkit and optional research support. You can run it on your computer or deploy it in your own cloud. This application does not require a hosted TACITUS account or subscription.

For a defined inquiry

Study claims, frames, values and possible prejudice by author, institution, document type and publication date. Compare named frameworks separately or state custom criteria for a specific research question.

Declare the reviewer’s perspective and intended audience. A report persona changes the emphasis, not the underlying source wording or the chosen analytical definition.

In your environment

Local SQLite and files hold a private workspace. Optional local inference keeps selected source text on your machine; a configured remote provider receives the text needed for that run.

Cloud deployments share the same analysis engine with durable storage and authenticated routing. Review provider terms and set a suitable budget before a remote run.

Interpretation remains reviewable. Exact-citation checks establish where words occur. They do not measure semantic correctness, determine an author’s private beliefs or turn a purposive collection into a public-opinion survey.
