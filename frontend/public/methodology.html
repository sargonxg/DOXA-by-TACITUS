A method to question,
not a verdict to inherit.

Make criteria, attribution, uncertainty and processing coverage explicit before interpreting a result.

Explicit criteria, separate frameworks.

IHRA, Jerusalem Declaration and Nexus are distinct analytical frameworks. Apply each according to its own stated criteria and scope. Anti-Muslim-prejudice, discourse and custom lenses also require a declared target and research question. DOXA does not collapse these into a universal prejudice score.

A criterion assessment records whether the passage meets the criterion, does not meet it, is uncertain or is not applicable. A failed or missing assessment cannot establish absence. Interpret “not detected” within the method and analyzed scope.

Attribution is part of the claim.

A prejudicial quotation can appear in a document opposing prejudice. Hypothetical training examples require the same care. Preserve the represented speaker, speech role, speaker stance, document stance and uncertainty alongside the passage.

Measure what was processed.

Keep the selected corpus, successfully assessed units, failed units and unextracted content distinct. Counts and rates must name their denominators. Source-block coverage and text-character coverage are different measures.

Comparisons require compatible criteria, model settings, source scope and time windows, or explicit disclosure of the differences. A purposive collection supports claims about that selection.

Validate interpretation separately.

Exact-quote validation checks passage location. Interpretation accuracy requires independently annotated examples and appropriate domain review. Review disagreements instead of treating model confidence as calibrated probability.

A model, rubric or reviewed-example update produces a new method version. Retain the baseline, held-out comparison and explicit activation decision.

Two histories, one auditable selection.

Publication-time changes may reflect different sources or collection practices. Analysis-time changes may reflect review, coverage or method updates. Neither automatically establishes a change in the underlying population.

Recorded showcase status. The original legacy v1.2 cloud run retained 1,071 observations across 20 documents, with 5,788 of 8,999 source blocks passing all checks (64.3%). It remains partial and lacks independent human labels. Those records are not validated results of the revised methodology.
